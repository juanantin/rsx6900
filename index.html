> SECTOR-01 // ABOUT

RSX6900 is reimagining the way holders become shareholders. Every trade of $RSX generates fees. Those fees are routed on-chain through a TheIndex Finance hook, which uses them to buy tokenized $RBLX stock. That stock is then airdropped, pro-rata, to every $RSX holder on a recurring distribution round. No staking, no claiming forms — hold $RSX in your wallet and the index does the rest.

01 · TRADE

Every buy and sell of $RSX on the Robinhood Chain generates a small fee, swept automatically into the index treasury.

02 · COMPOUND

The TheIndex Finance hook converts collected fees into tokenized $RBLX stock, compounding the treasury round over round.

03 · AIRDROP

At the end of each distribution round, accumulated $RBLX is airdropped directly to $RSX holders' wallets — automatically.
